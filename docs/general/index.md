# General

General resources for making small games.

## MakeCode Arcade Tools

### Pixel Passport (Proof Of Concept)

Create a 16×16 pixel monster, distribute its stats, and generate NFC data for use with MakeCode Arcade.

<a href="/pixel-passport-editor">Open Pixel Passport Editor</a>

### Pixel Passport Card Maker (Proof Of Concept)

Turn your Pixel Passport monsters into collectible trading cards! Enter your monster's name and NFC data to generate a custom Water, Fire, or Grass card. Download or print the front and back of your card.

<a href="/pixel-passport-card-maker">Open Pixel Passport Card Maker</a>
