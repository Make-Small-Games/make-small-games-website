Monster Card Maker

Turn your Pixel Passport monster into a collectible card. Add its name, generate a Water, Fire, or Grass design, and print both sides at real trading-card size.

1. Create your card

0 / 269 characters

Accepts the exact 269-character format from your editor: 256 pixels + facing + type + 3 stats + 8 reserved characters. Optional PPSTART/ZZSTART prefixes are also accepted.

The name is printed on both the front and back.

Paste a code or load the sample to begin.

2. Print and cut

Use US Letter paper, print at Actual size / 100%, and disable browser headers and footers. The card measures 2.5 × 3.5 inches.

Fold-and-glue: Cut around the 5 × 3.5 inch outline. Fold the right side behind the left with both printed designs facing outward, then glue or laminate. The dashed center line is your fold guide.
