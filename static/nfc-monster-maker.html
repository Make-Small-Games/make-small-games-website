NFC Data

How it works

Each pixel becomes one hexadecimal character from 0-F. That gives the sprite exactly 256 characters.

NFC data:
PIXELS|H=#|A=#|S=#|T=#

T= Type

B= Black Magic
W= White Magic
R= Rainbow Magic

Every monster starts at:
Health 1 · Attack 1 · Speed 1

Then the player spends exactly 10 additional points.

HP = 120 + ((Health - 1) × 5)
Damage = 20 + (Attack - 1)
Dodge = 10 + ((Speed - 1) × 3%)
Max Dodge = 40%

Speed also determines who attacks first. The NFC stores the three stat points, not the calculated combat values, so you can rebalance the game later without rewriting old cards.
